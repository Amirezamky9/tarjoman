Hearken unto the words of antiquity, for the ancient chronicle doth declare the sovereign decrees of old. From generation unto generation, thy covenant hath stood unbroken amid the transient kingdoms of dust.

Wherefore dost thou despair when darkness encompasses the land? The noble knight feareth no shadow, for divine providence hath ordained his path through the wilderness.

"Bow thine ear and listen," spoke the solemn prophet, "for thou art chosen to bear witness unto the sacred wisdom inscribed upon these timeless tablets of stone."
