GENEVA (Reuters) — In breaking news from the international summit, diplomatic envoys reached a landmark ceasefire agreement following an intensive press briefing late Thursday evening.

According to sources close to the negotiations, the accord establishes an immediate demilitarized corridor for humanitarian assistance. A senior spokesperson for the delegation reported that all participating governments had endorsed the framework, calling it a decisive turning point in regional peace efforts.

"This breakthrough marks a renewed commitment to multilateral cooperation," the spokesperson stated during the live broadcast. Correspondent reports from Associated Press confirmed that official documentation will be signed tomorrow, dominating global headline coverage.
